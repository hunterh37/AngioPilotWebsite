Fly the vasculature.

AngioPilot is a visionOS medical simulation of vascular catheterization surgery. Trainees pilot catheter and wire from access to target in full spatial 3D, with scenario branching, contrast runs, and complication drills.

  • Access: Femoral artery puncture, sheath placement, hemostasis
  • Navigation: Aortic arch, celiac trunk, splenic artery selection
  • Judgment: Contrast dose, wire choice, bailout decisions scored per run

How a run works

  1. Brief. Indication, imaging, kit check.
  2. Access. Ultrasound-guided stick in spatial view.
  3. Navigate. Wire ahead, catheter follow, roadmapping.
  4. Treat & close. Target run, assessment, debrief score.
