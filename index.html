Overview

AngioPilot is a scenario-based simulation trainer for vascular catheterization, built for Apple Vision Pro. Practitioners fly a guidewire and catheter from the femoral access point to a target vessel, rehearsing route selection, device handling, and contrast timing before they ever reach the table.
