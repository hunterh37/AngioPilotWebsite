Fly the vasculature.

AngioPilot is a visionOS medical simulation of vascular catheterization surgery. Trainees pilot catheter and wire from access to target in full spatial 3D, with scenario branching, contrast runs, and complication drills.

  • AccessFemoral punctureSheath placement and hemostasis.
  • NavigationArch to targetAortic arch, celiac trunk, splenic selection.
  • JudgmentScored per runContrast dose, wire choice, bailout calls.

Itinerary

How a run works

  1. Brief. Indication, imaging review, kit check before the wire moves.
  2. Access. Ultrasound-guided stick performed in spatial view.
  3. Navigate. Wire ahead, catheter follow, roadmapping through the arch.
  4. Treat & close. Target run, assessment, and a scored debrief.
